# Plan

What should happen, one bullet per line, in the floor plan's timezone.
`office meeting` writes a meeting here from your words. A bullet you
write by hand in one of these shapes is read the same way, a one-off and
a series (DAY is mon to sun, day or weekday):

    - YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM title [about: alpha, beta]
    - every DAY HH:MM title [about: alpha]

`[about: ...]` names the projects a meeting concerns (`the office` for
the office itself). `office week` lays this page against the record.
