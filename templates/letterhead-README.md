# The letterhead

How anything that leaves this office must look and sound: the words a
delivery carries, a commit, a review request, a message written in the
occupant's voice. The office keeps it; the occupant's words fill it.

- voice.md, the voice page: the occupant's style rule for text that
  leaves the office, in the occupant's words. A session that writes text
  for a project with an audience reads it before it writes, and writes
  to it.
- forbidden.txt, the forbidden list: one fixed string per line, refused
  by `office letterhead check` and by `office deliver` before anything
  is pushed. The em dash, the en dash and the ellipsis character are
  refused in this office by default, with no line needed; a `lift:` line
  there lifts one by name, and every check says so.

The check's own list (the names of models, vendors and products,
tell-tale phrasing, bale's marks) is the install's, share/letterhead.toml.
This office adds to it here, and never edits it.
