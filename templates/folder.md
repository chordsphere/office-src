One job. Open folders sit on the desk; closing one moves it to the
filing cabinet. Named by date and a short slug, and cited as folder N
by the number office open minted for it. The lines above are the
folder's facts, one field per line, written by the office command and
never by hand: its number, its status, the day it was queued, and
whichever of class, size, lane, source, blueprint, links and gates it
was given. A field the command cannot fill is left out rather than
left blank.

## Asked

Who asked, when, through what, and what they asked for, in their words
when there are words to keep.

## Questions and answers

The questions the job raised and the occupant's answers, dated.

## Correspondence

Messages about the job, including copies of what was sent.

## Deliverables

What the job produced and where it lives. A thing with an audience lives
in its project; this names the project and path.

## Concerns

Projects, handbook chapters and people this job touches.

## Sessions

The ids of the sessions that worked on this job, one per line, with a
few words each.

## Next

What happens next, and who moves.

## Riders

Riders this folder's next session carries, each with its text, moved
in from the drafting table's riders page.

## History

One event per line, dated, appended and never rewritten: what happened
to this folder and when. The status line above always agrees with the
last state-changing line here.
