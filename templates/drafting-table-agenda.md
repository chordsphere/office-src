# Agenda

Rulings awaiting a sitting: decisions, not work. One entry per
question, numbered `agenda N`, with where it came from and the folders
waiting on it. A sitting that rules on it appends the ruling to
rulings.md, removes the entry from this page, and leaves one dated line
under Disposed at the foot of the page. A folder that must wait for the
decision gates on it (`after: agenda N`). Numbers are minted in order
and never reused, disposed entries included. An entry, and the line it
leaves behind (values illustrative):

```markdown
## agenda 7 - Where a chat lands

- source: notepad/2026-09-28-seed-sitting.md
- waiting: folder 141, folder 143

The question, in a line or two, and what turns on it.
```

```markdown
- 2026-10-02 agenda 7 -> ruling 2026-10-02-riders-home
```
